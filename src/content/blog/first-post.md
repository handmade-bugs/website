I'm not really sure how to start.

In a nutshell, I'd like to have a space where I can share my thoughts, ideas and things I have learnt (yes, I'm still trying to learn things in the age of AI).

Let me explain.

I remember watching a TedX talk called ["start with why"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4ZoJKF_VuA), which I really liked, I even bought the book to read more about this approach. Unfortunately, it turned out to be 270 pages of pretty much the same content - it was a bit annoying because I'm a slow reader, and I get distracted quite easily.

That said, this approach worked for me quite well many times before, so I'll try to apply it again here.

## Why

I was always a perfectionist (and for many years I thought it's a good trait to have, oh how wrong I was) and I would spend a lot of time planning, analysing and making sure that whatever I deliver was good enough. That's one of the reasons that I never had an open source project - a simple todo app idea to experiment with a technology would become a project that I would want to monetise, and weeks later I would end up with a Trello board with design inspirations, competitor analysis and a backlog of features impossible to deliver by myself.

I really like the idea of having a space where I can talk about what I've learnt, share my ideas or just rant. I honestly don't mind if no one reads this - the reason for doing this is simply to clear my head. It's like when you feel overwhelmed with all the things you have to do, but as soon as you create a todo list, you suddenly feel much calmer.

## How

I could try and learn how to deal with my overthinking, that would be the right thing to do... but I'm very impatient. I just want to play around with tech and talk about it.

I want to start, like now. I don't have the headspace to deal with my overthinking, so I decided to just keep this anonymous (which actually feels quite liberating!)

## What

Well, I don't really know what this will be, but that's fine. It's going to be a blog/diary with tech(ish) content.

I don't have enough spare time to go through the normal design and development process, so I'm just releasing a basic HTML and CSS, and I'll improve it whenever I can.

All the code is on [Github](https://github.com/handmade-bugs/website).

That's it for now!
